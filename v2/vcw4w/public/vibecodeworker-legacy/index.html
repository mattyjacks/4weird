A CLEARER LOOP

From a vague feeling to a useful next step.

VibeCodeWorker keeps the everyday loop small. Detailed controls are there when you need them, not when you are simply trying to get started.

  1. 1

    Point it at the work

    Load a local page, a game, or a URL. Keep the live viewport and the target in one place.

  2. 2

    Name the outcome

    Use plain language: test menus, find visual glitches, check controls, or describe a custom flow.

  3. 3

    Run and observe

    Collect the action trail, performance signals, screenshots, console context, and potential defects.

  4. 4

    Investigate with context

    Inspect source, review bugs, save replays, or prepare a repair workflow when you need more depth.
