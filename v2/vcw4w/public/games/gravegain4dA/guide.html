🌀 4D CONCEPTS IN PLAIN WORDS

You never see four dimensions — you see a 3D slice of a 4D world. Everything below is that idea in golfer words.

↔️
Ana & Kata
The 4th Direction (W)
Ana and kata are the two ends of the fourth direction — like left/right, but sideways from all of 3D at once.

Your ball lives in 4D (x, y, z, w). The Q / E keys slide the world along W: one way is ana, the other is kata. A putt with a little W-curl leaves our slice and re-enters further along — in 3D it looks like the ball folds through itself. Dream it as turning the page the green is printed on.

🥞
Branes (Slices)
Light / Gloom
A brane is one 3D slice of the 4D green — the page you are currently playing on. Light and Gloom are two such pages.

The playable world is a hyperplane section at position w (the engine calls it sliceT). Sliding w morphs the fairway: cube → strange truncated shapes → cube. Pressing B breaches between the Light brane and the Gloom brane when the rift allows it — walls, hazards, and echoes differ per side, so breach to find the clean line.

👻
Ghost Echoes
Your Parallel Self
Your echo shows what your parallel self just did — read it before you move.

When the Array breached the Citadel, MoonRock folded into parallel timelines. Your ghost echo replays your last line through the fold. If the echo eats a wall, change your aim, power, or W-shift — don't repeat it.

⏳ TIMEFOLD STRATEGY

Time travel here is just a stroll along +W. Rewinds never destroy history — they branch the present.

⏪
Spend Rewinds Late
Golden Rule
One rewind per descent — spend it late, when the fold turns against you, not on the first stumble.

Fold Cartographer Vex's rule. Early holes (1–3) are for learning the echo; save chrono-sand for par-5 grinds (holes 4, 8, 9, 10) where one bad fold costs a full stroke budget. Expand chrono-sand capacity at the hub before the back nine.

🎯
Paint the Overlap
Fold-Then-Strike
Rotate the fold until the boss's echo overlaps its body — then strike the overlap to hit every fork at once.

Each mission has a fold lens (XW, YW, ZW, or the full tesseract on hole 10). Work the Q / E shift first and watch the echo converge; only charge the putt (click-hold) once echo and body agree. This is the orbital-strike trick from Mission 8 and the only way to corner Hades in Mission 10.

🌉
Breach to Purge
Brane Scouting
When a green looks poisoned, breach (B) and compare — purge what differs.

President Good's catacombs lesson: the clean garden overlays the toxic one across the fold. Breach to the Gloom brane, note which hazards vanish, breach back, and aim the putt through the clean line. Costs no chrono-sand — scout freely.

💡 TIPS

Fold wisdom from Vex, Sable, and the Ossuary Twins.

🌀
Read Before You Move
Vex's Law
The W-slice drifts — your ghost echo shows where the fold goes if you commit.

Shift Q / E first, watch the echo, then charge the putt. The dungeon punishes the careless and rewards the patient.

🗺️
Mark Your Exits
Sable's Rule
The fold doubles every dead end — scout both branes before spending sand.

Breach (B) is free; rewinds (T) are not. Map the hole on both slices, then commit the stroke budget.

💀
Adds First, Boss Last
Twins' Warning
Everyone the dungeon takes ends up in the walls — thin the pile before the putt that matters.

Every boss from hole 2 onward is screened by adds (up to 40 guardians + phased spawns on holes 9–10). Clear screens, overload conduits on transitions, and never follow the whispering cracks — count your strokes.
