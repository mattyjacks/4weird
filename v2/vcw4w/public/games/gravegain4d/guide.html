GraveGain4D · Player Guide

4D golf in the GraveGain canon. Putt through a 3D slice of tesseract dungeons.

GraveGain4D tees off from the MoonRock colony aboard the LuckyStarShip: 10 holes of time-bent golf. You never see all four dimensions at once — you play a 3D slice of a 4D dungeon (a tesseract course), and steer the slice itself to bend the fairway home.

1 · How a hole works

⛳ Aim & launch

Drag the mouse to aim the shot arc, release to launch. Tap Space for a short putt near the cup.

🌀 W-slice

Press Q / E to rotate the 3D slice along the W-axis (the fourth direction). Same ball, same course — a different cross-section, so blocked paths open and hazards slide away.

⏪ Timelines & rewind

Press R to rewind your last shot — informational time travel, GraveGain-style: the ball returns, the lesson stays. No score penalty beyond the lost stroke.

⏸ Pause

Press P (or Esc) to pause. Touch players: use the on-screen buttons.

3 · W-slice & timelines, in plain words

The course is 4D; you see 3D. A tesseract dungeon has four axes (X, Y, Z, W). Your screen shows the X/Y/Z slice at one W position. Q/E slides that slice along W — walls thin out, cups drift closer, hazards phase through the floor. Think of it as changing which "floor" of the hypercube you are standing on.

Timelines are your mulligan. R rewinds the ball to before your last launch. The dungeon keeps its generated shape (par 3–5, 2–5 hazards per hole), so your second attempt is genuinely smarter: slice first, then shoot.

Beginner loop: aim → launch → missed? → R rewind → Q/E slice → shoot again → Space to finish.

5 · Lore primer (no spoilers beyond the guide)

🌙 MoonRock

MoonRock is the grandest moon of the ringed giant Giantess, orbiting the star FarStar — home to elven glow-forests, dwarven highland mines, and orc southern wastes.

🚀 LuckyStarShip

The colony ship that carried 50,000 sleepers across two centuries. Now your clubhouse, orbiting above the course.

🔮 MERCENARY

The machine-mind that dreams backward through time. It moves no matter — only knowledge, sent as dreams. Your rewinds are its small mercies.

🌿 President Angel Good

Botanist-turned-president who bound four peoples into one alliance and runs the ship's botany deck.

👻 Elder Mirathiel

Elven seer and keeper of the Mother Trees, who felt the dead rise through the roots before any instrument did.

👹 Warchief Groknak

Orc ally who sealed the alliance in mixed blood. His standing order: orcs do not die sitting down.

👑 Hades & the Array

Dr. Lucifer Hades stayed awake for the whole voyage building the Necromantic Array — signal towers on the ley-line crossings that woke every corpse on Day 7. The Array's full burn tore the W-axis wound your fairways leak through.

🌀 The W-axis wound

Ten holes, ten echoes of ten fights. Play them, close them. That is the whole mission.

6 · Tips

  • Slice before you shoot: Q/E first, then aim. Most "impossible" holes are one W-step from easy.
  • Rewind is free learning — use R aggressively on holes 6–9.
  • Putt with Space inside the green ring; full launches overshoot the cup.
  • On touch: short drags for putts, long drags for drives.
